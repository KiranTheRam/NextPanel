A bold simple violet arrow points RIGHT, positioned toward the BOTTOM of the page, crossing over the page's lower area and extending visibly past its right edge into the dark background. The arrow must clearly extend OFF the page to the right. Keep the arrow below the comic panels, so the empty panel layout remains clearly recognizable. Page is upright, no folded corner, no perspective. Very simple geometric forms, consistent stroke weights, balanced spacing, readable at 32px. Flat colors ONLY, no gradients, no shadows, no highlights, no 3D, no textures, no extraneous embellishments, no bookmark, no letters, no text or watermark. Single centered symbol with ample margins; full-bleed opaque square background, no outer rounded border or mockup. Icon page-and-arrow group contained within central 65 percent of canvas.
